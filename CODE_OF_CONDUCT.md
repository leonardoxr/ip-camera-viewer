# Code Of Conduct

This project should be a practical, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, but respond well when impact is pointed out.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Respect privacy when discussing cameras, homes, offices, and local networks.
- Avoid posting screenshots, logs, or network details that expose private spaces or credentials.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sharing real camera credentials or private stream URLs.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior in issues, discussions, or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; or block participants who make the project unsafe or unproductive.
